Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026

Resume Genius processes resume text only to provide the analysis requested by the user. Resume content may include personal and professional information.

How information is processed

Uploaded files are read in the user’s browser. Extracted text is sent through the Resume Genius backend to the configured AI provider to identify skills, research opportunities, and generate results.

What users should upload

Remove Social Security numbers, government identifiers, financial information, medical information, security-clearance details, and other information that is unnecessary for career analysis. Only upload a resume you own or have permission to process.

Storage

This version does not intentionally save uploaded resume files or analysis results to an application database. Infrastructure and AI providers may process operational logs according to their applicable policies.

Accuracy and deletion

AI-generated results may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be independently verified. Closing or resetting the page clears the visible session state; it does not control third-party infrastructure logs.

Contact

Privacy questions may be directed to the application owner through the official PressCreates contact channel.
